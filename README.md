# Atlas Observatory

Atlas Observatory is an independent open-source initiative dedicated to advancing transparent, reproducible, and accessible public data. Our mission is to transform dispersed public information into research-grade datasets, analytical tools, and digital infrastructure that support rigorous scholarship, evidence-based policymaking, and public understanding.

---

## Mission

To build sustainable research infrastructure that enables the collection, standardization, validation, documentation, and dissemination of high-quality public data.

---

## Vision

To become a trusted platform for open data, computational research, and reproducible analysis across developing economies.

---

## Current Projects

###  Atlas Data Toolkit (ADT)

An open-source toolkit for extracting, cleaning, validating, and standardizing structured data from government publications.

**Status:** Under active development.

---

### 📊 Nepal Economic Database

A curated collection of research-grade macroeconomic and socioeconomic datasets accompanied by comprehensive metadata, documentation, and source references.

**Status:** Planned.

---

### 🌐 Atlas Observatory Platform

A public platform providing interactive visualizations, downloadable datasets, technical documentation, and research outputs.

**Status:** Planned.

---

## Core Principles

- Transparency
- Reproducibility
- Scientific Integrity
- Open Knowledge
- Data Preservation
- Long-term Sustainability

---

## Research Philosophy

Reliable evidence begins with reliable data. Atlas Observatory promotes rigorous data curation, transparent methodologies, and reproducible research practices to strengthen academic inquiry and public policy.
